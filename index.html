About

I am a PhD candidate in computational chemistry at the Institut Charles Gerhardt Montpellier (CNRS / University of Montpellier), working with Guillaume Maurin. Through a CIFRE industrial PhD, I am also a research engineer in the CO₂ and Sustainability R&D team at TotalEnergies in Pau, working with Yann Magnin.

My work asks two questions: which metal–organic frameworks (MOFs) can separate industrial gas mixtures such as CH₄/H₂, N₂/H₂ and CO₂/CH₄, and what happens to a MOF once it is shaped into a pellet with a polymer binder. I use Monte Carlo and molecular dynamics simulations, density functional theory and machine-learned potentials, and compare the results with measurements from experimental collaborators.

Before the PhD, I spent four years at TCS Research in Pune building simulation and machine-learning models for small molecules: how they cross lipid membranes, and which candidates are likely to taste umami or carry a given odor. That work was done with clients in food, cosmetics and pharmaceuticals.

I expect to defend in September 2027 and am open to positions in both academia and industry.
